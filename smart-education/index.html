Why schools switch

Everything NEP-aligned digital governance asks for, minus the paperwork

Three portals, one shared record — so a mark entered by an educator, a fee paid by a parent, and a doubt asked by a student all land in the same place.

Attendance in under a minute

Teachers mark a whole class from a tap-friendly roll call; absentees trigger an automatic note to parents.

Holistic, competency-based report cards

Enter marks once per test; term report cards, class rank, and subject averages assemble themselves — in line with NEP 2020's shift away from rote marks alone.

An AI tutor for every student

Students ask questions in English or Hindi and get worked explanations that follow the school's own syllabus — supporting NEP 2020's push for mother-tongue access.

Fee ledger with due-date reminders

Admins see collections by class in real time, and families get a reminder before a due date — not after.

Lesson plans educators can reuse

Faculty file a lesson plan once; it's ready for the next section or the next academic year with a copy, not a rewrite.

One dashboard, whole-school view

Principals see enrolment, attendance trends, and exam performance by class without asking three different people.
